Gamepad: left stick or d-pad aims, A confirms (hold A to keep walking), LB / RB turn the view.
Keyboard: arrows or WASD aim (two keys for a diagonal), Space or Enter confirms, Q / E turn the view by 45 degrees, R / F tilt it up and down.
Mouse: point at a cell to see the way, click to go there; the wheel zooms, the right or middle button drags the view.
Keyboard + / - zoom. The view goes back to the player when the player moves.
Walk into a monster to attack it, into a closed door to open it.
